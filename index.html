How it works

Picking

Something's not working?
First try Settings → Reset the app on this phone. It clears the app's saved data and reloads it, and your picks are safe. Still broken? In Settings, use Report a problem. Describe what happened and tap Send; Paul gets it right away.
Where are notification settings?
Tap Settings (the slider button at the top) to turn notifications on or off and choose which ones you get.
How do I make a pick?
Tap the team you think will win. It saves on its own; you'll see Saving… for a second. Regular-season games also have a small Tie button for the rare game that ends tied.
Can I change my pick?
Yes. Tap the other team any time until that game kicks off.
When do picks lock?
Each game locks on its own at its scheduled kickoff time. Thursday's game locks Thursday, Sunday's games lock when each one starts. If a game starts late, it still locks at the listed time. If the NFL moves a game, the lock time moves with it.
When can I see everyone else's picks?
On each game, once you've made your pick. Before that you only see how many people have picked. Once a game kicks off, everyone's picks for it are shown.

Scoring

How is it scored?
Pick the winner and it's a win; pick the loser and it's a loss. If a game ends in a tie, only a Tie pick wins. No pick means it just doesn't count.
How are people ranked?
On the Stats tab, by win percentage for whatever range you pick, then total wins. Joining partway through the season doesn't hurt you.
What's on the Group tab?
Everyone's picks for the week in one grid. A ✓ means that person has picked but you can't see it yet; make your own pick on that game (or wait for kickoff) to see theirs.
What's on the Stats tab?
Standings (with perfect weeks), week-by-week records and splits. Use the menus to pick a season and a range (one week, a span of weeks, regular season, playoffs), and tap People to choose who's included. Only games that have kicked off count.
What are "upsets" and "with group"?
Upsets: correct picks of the team with the worse record going into the game. With group: how often your pick matched what most of the selected people picked.
When do scores update?
Automatically about every 30 minutes, and records update when games go final. Tap ↻ at the top to refresh now.
How do I see past weeks?
Use the week dropdown at the top.

The app

How do I put this on my home screen?
Android: open your link in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.
iPhone: open your link in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Can I change my name?
Yes. Tap Settings, change Your name, and tap Save. Everyone sees the new name right away, and your picks and records stay with you.
What are groups?
Paul can put people in groups (like Greco's or Friends). Picks, Group and Stats open on your group; use the Group menu at the top of each tab to switch to another group or Everyone. Each group gets its own 👑 and 💩.
How do I see someone's profile?
Tap their name on the Group tab or in the Stats standings to see their groups, record and any 👑/💩. Tap Hi, (your name) at the top to see your own.
Someone else wants to play?
Settings → Request a new player. Type their name (and an optional note) and send it to Paul. He adds them and sends their link; you'll get a notification when they're in.
Can I hide someone?
Yes, just for you: Settings → Hide people. They disappear from your Picks, Group and Stats screens. Nobody else is affected, and you can show them again any time.
What's the tiebreaker?
The last game of each week has a Tiebreaker box: guess the total points scored. If two or more people tie for the week's 👑, the closest guess takes it. Guesses lock at that game's kickoff and everyone's show up then.
Reactions?
Once a game kicks off, tap an emoji under it (😂 🔥 💀 🤡 😤 👀). Tap again to take it back. Everyone sees who reacted.
Weekly recap?
Once a week is final (usually after Monday night), a 🗞️ recap button appears at the top of Picks until the next week's first kickoff. First your week (record, rank, best call, the one that got away), then your group's week: final standings with 👑 and 💩, biggest upset, the game everyone got wrong, the lock of the week, the most reacted game, and season-so-far highlights. Tap ✕ to hide it.
Light or dark mode? Team colors?
Settings → Appearance: Auto (follows your phone), Light or Dark, plus Team colors for every NFL team and a bunch of Eagles looks (Midnight Green, Black, Silver, Kelly Green, Kelly White and the Frankford Yellow Jackets throwback). It's just for your phone.
What's Wrapped? 🎁
Your season recap, Spotify-style: record and rank, best and worst weeks, perfect weeks, your ride-or-die team, your nemesis, upsets called, lone-wolf moments, your pick twin and more. It shows up at the top of the Stats tab late in the season (Week 15 on), and everyone gets a notification once the Super Bowl is final. Tap the right side to go forward, the left side to go back. It uses the group selected at the top.
What's an upset alert?
In groups of 3 or more, when only one person picked the winner (and at least two others didn't), you get one roundup after that day's games are done, like "2 upsets today in the Greco group: Holly took the Jets; Mom took the Bears." Turn it off in Settings.
What are the 👑 and 💩?
👑 goes to the winner of the most recent finished week (most wins that week). 💩 goes to whoever had the fewest wins that week, among people who picked. Ties share. They move to the new winners when the next week is final.
New phone, or it says to open my link?
Just open your personal link again. That's how the app knows who you are. Lost it? Ask Paul.

Admin (only you see this)

Add someone
Tap Settings → Manage people, type their name and tap Add. Then send them their link with Send link. You can grab anyone's link there any time.
Player requests
When someone asks to add a person, you get a notification and a 🔔 Review banner on Picks. In Settings → Manage people, the request shows at the top: fix the name or groups if you want (it suggests the requester's group), then Approve & add (their link opens to send) or Decline. The requester is told when you approve.
Remind someone to pick
Tap their name (Group tab or Stats) and tap 🔔 Remind … to pick, or use 🔔 Remind everyone with open picks at the top of Settings → Manage people. It goes out right away, even if they turned pick reminders off, and only to people who still have open picks. Once per person every 10 minutes.
Groups
In Settings → Manage people, type someone's groups separated by commas (e.g. Greco's, Friends) and tap Save. Their first group is the one their app opens on. To take someone out of a group, delete it from their list and Save. You're in every group automatically.
Someone stopped playing
In Settings → Manage people, tap Retire (twice to confirm). They disappear for everyone and their link stops working, but their past picks stay in Stats. Bring back undoes it.
Fix or add a pick after kickoff
Open Admin override on that game's card, pick the team (or Missed, which counts as a loss) for that person, and tap Save. It's marked "override."
Someone's link got shared
In Settings → Manage people, tap New link next to them (tap twice to confirm) and send them the new one. The old link stops working; their picks stay.
Changing the script
After saving Code.gs, use Deploy → Manage deployments → pencil → New version → Deploy. Never New deployment; that changes the address the app uses.
